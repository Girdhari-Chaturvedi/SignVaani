Languages

Voice quality

For the clearest voice: on Windows open Microsoft Edge (it has “Natural” online voices, often including Hindi, Gujarati and Odia). On Android install the voice for your language in Settings → Text-to-speech → Google. On iPhone go to Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices and pick an “Enhanced” voice. Then select that voice above.

More languages

Built-in speech languages: Hindi, English, Gujarati, Odia and Awadhi (Awadhi uses the Hindi voice). To verify a translation or add another language (e.g. Marathi), export a template, fill in the words, and import it back.
